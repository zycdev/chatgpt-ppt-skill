# Example — Post-sample slide with a strict source figure

# Slide 8 — 实验结果｜持续实践带来可保持的能力增益

## Canvas
- One complete 16:9 PowerPoint slide image.
- Simplified Chinese.
- No slide number, watermark, or invented citation.

## Slide intent
Use the supplied experiment figure as the main evidence. The slide should communicate one conclusion clearly: performance improves after continual practice while historical capability remains substantially retained.

## Input images
- **Image 1 — approved sample slide; style reference only.** Match visual identity, not layout or content.
- **Image 2 — experiment result figure; strict input asset.** It must appear visibly and remain semantically unchanged.

## Strict asset fidelity for Image 2
Preserve Image 2's:
- axes;
- legends;
- labels;
- numerical values;
- line/bar ordering;
- colors that encode series;
- relative geometry and trends.

Do not redraw, reinterpret, relabel, “clean up,” or synthesize a replacement chart. Compose the surrounding title, callouts, background, and conclusion around the supplied figure.

## Exact visible text
**Title**
实验结果｜持续实践带来可保持的能力增益

**Callout 1**
综合任务性能持续提升

**Callout 2**
历史能力保持率 ≥ 90%

**Takeaway**
新知识积累与既有能力保持可以同时实现

## Composition
Make Image 2 the dominant evidence panel, using roughly half to two-thirds of the usable content area. Place 2 concise callouts beside the figure with thin leader lines that terminate outside the figure's important data region. Add a small takeaway strip or open flat-color zone near the bottom.

Do not shrink the figure merely to make room for decoration.

## Text repairability
All newly rendered text must sit on uniform solid-color local backgrounds with generous clear space. No leader line, chart mark, texture, or decoration may pass behind text. Ensure each text block could later be covered by a flat rectangle about 20% larger without obscuring Image 2 or damaging nearby graphics.

## Hard constraints
- Image 2 is evidence, not style inspiration.
- Maintain the approved sample's visual language.
- Do not modify the source figure's semantic content.
- Keep the conclusion visually subordinate to the actual evidence.

## Negative constraints
- No recreated chart.
- No invented data labels.
- No decorative pseudo-statistics.
- No unrelated icons.
- No gradient or textured text background.
- No line crossing any text.

## Acceptance criteria
Accept only if the source figure remains recognizable and faithful, all visible text is correct, and the callouts clarify rather than obscure the evidence.
