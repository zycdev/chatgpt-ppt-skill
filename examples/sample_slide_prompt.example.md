## Semantic relationships
The visual must communicate a real loop rather than five independent modules:

- the current question defines an information need;
- retrieval brings candidate evidence;
- reading evaluates whether the evidence resolves the current need;
- unresolved gaps trigger query reformulation;
- the system either loops again or stops when evidence is sufficient.

## Composition
Create one dominant **left-to-right process with a visible feedback loop**.

Suggested rhythm:
- left: “问题理解” as the entry;
- center: “证据检索 → 证据阅读” as the main evidence path;
- right: “终止判断” as a clear decision point;
- a feedback arrow from unresolved evidence to “查询改写”, then back toward retrieval;
- the bottom takeaway sits in open flat space.

Use one coherent process diagram. Do not turn the five stages into a generic row of identical cards.

## Text repairability
- All visible text, including the title, sits on white or flat solid-color local backgrounds.
- No arrow, connector, gradient, texture, icon, or illustration detail passes behind text.
- Long labels receive generous padding.
- Keep labels far enough from arrows that a rectangle about 20% larger than each label could cover it without damaging the diagram.
- Use open white space rather than putting every label in a boxed card.
