# Slide 4 — 核心研究框架｜从实践中形成可持续演进的能力

## Canvas
- One complete 16:9 PowerPoint slide image.
- Language: Simplified Chinese.
- Do not render a slide number, watermark, fake citation, or unrelated logo.

## Deck goal and audience
Formal research-project defense for an expert review panel. The deck explains how a single autonomous agent improves through repeated interaction with its environment. This representative sample must establish the deck's visual identity while also proving that the style can handle a non-trivial conceptual diagram.

## Slide role and intent
This is the deck's **representative sample slide**.

It should make one core idea immediately clear:

**已有认知指导行动，实践产生新证据，学习再更新认知，形成持续循环。**

The page must demonstrate the intended typography, spacing, diagram language, density, color discipline, and repair-safe treatment that later slides will inherit.

## Selected detailed style
Use `references/科研答辩风.md` together with the approved `visual_spec.md`.

Interpret the style as a modern academic-defense system:
- light background;
- deep academic blue for structure;
- limited warm/red emphasis for decisive concepts;
- evidence-first, mechanism-driven layout;
- no commercial pitch-deck gloss;
- no repetitive equal-card dashboard.

## Exact visible text

**Title**
核心研究框架｜从实践中形成可持续演进的能力

**Three core capabilities**
认知的可行动性
实践的认知增益性
学习的累进性

**Loop labels**
认知 → 行动 → 证据 → 学习 → 更新认知

**Bottom takeaway**
知以导行 · 行以致知 · 知行相长

Do not add filler paragraphs.

## Semantic relationships
The visual must communicate a closed-loop system rather than three independent modules:

- current cognition influences action;
- action occurs in an environment and produces evidence/feedback;
- learning interprets that evidence and updates cognition;
- updated cognition changes later action;
- the three capabilities should map naturally onto this loop.

## Composition
Create one dominant **loop / cyclic mechanism** occupying the central visual field.

A suitable composition:
- center: simplified autonomous-agent/core cognition symbol;
- around it: three major stages/capabilities arranged as a coherent loop;
- arrows show the direction of action, evidence, and learning;
- a restrained environment boundary or contextual field can surround the loop;
- the bottom takeaway sits in open flat space and visually closes the page.

Do not use three disconnected same-size cards.

## Text repairability
- All visible text sits on white or flat solid-color local backgrounds.
- No arrow, connector, gradient, texture, icon, or illustration detail passes behind text.
- Long labels receive generous padding.
- Keep the three capability labels far enough from arrows that a rectangle about 20% larger than each label could cover it without damaging the diagram.
- Use open white space rather than putting every label in a boxed card.

## Hard constraints
- Full-slide native ImageGen artwork.
- This slide must be strong enough to serve as the primary style anchor for the rest of the deck.
- Exact Chinese terminology.
- Formal, modern research-defense tone.
- One clear reading order and one dominant visual story.
- Medium information density; no paragraph blocks.

## Negative constraints
- No generic AI robot portrait.
- No neon/cyberpunk effects.
- No glossy 3D icon set.
- No fake citations or institution logos.
- No decorative microtext.
- No arrows crossing any text.
- No gradient/texture/photo behind text.
- No repeated-card dashboard look.

## Acceptance criteria
Show this sample to the user only if:
- all required Chinese text is correct and legible;
- the loop logic is visually obvious;
- the page demonstrates a reusable visual system rather than a one-off poster;
- all text regions pass the repairability overlay test;
- the composition leaves room for later slides to vary while retaining the same visual identity.
